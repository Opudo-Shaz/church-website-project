Our Story

Higher Life Church

We are a Bible believing church who love the Lord and are committed to living out our faith through the lens of God's Word. We are dedicated to providing a welcoming environment where people can grow in God's Word and in their relationship with the Lord.

Vision

Unveiling our Lord Jesus to the world.

Mission

Living the higher life in Christ.
